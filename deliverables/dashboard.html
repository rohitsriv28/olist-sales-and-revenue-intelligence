Regional Logistics Disparities

While the economic heartland of São Paulo (SP) boasts an 8.8-day delivery average and 94% on-time fulfillment, far northern/northeastern states like Alagoas (AL) and Roraima (RR) face delivery cycles exceeding 24 to 29 days, directly threatening customer retention.

The 1-Star Review “Satisfaction Cliff”

Orders delivered on time receive an overwhelming 82.7% positive ratings (4 or 5 stars) and just 6.5% 1-star ratings. In stark contrast, late deliveries plunge into a 46.2% 1-star satisfaction cliff (an ~7× surge in dissatisfied reviews). Carrier speed is the #1 driver of negative churn.
